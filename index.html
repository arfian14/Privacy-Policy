Privacy Policy

This privacy notice applies to the Moneza: Budget & Money Manager app developed by Arfiansyah Dev ("we," "us," or "our"). It explains how your data is collected, stored, and used when using our services.

By using the application, you acknowledge and agree to this privacy policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at arfiansyahdev@gmail.com.

What Information Do We Collect?

Moneza operates entirely offline, and all financial data, including transaction records, budgets, and expense categories, is stored only on your device. We do not collect or process this data on external servers. However, if Google Play Store’s automatic backup feature is enabled, your data may be backed up to your Google account, which is managed solely by Google.

Additionally, we collect:

  • Crash and Log Data: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic logs to improve app stability. This may include device model, OS version, and error logs but does not contain financial data.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect non-personal data like device information and ad interactions to serve relevant ads. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Feedback Data: If you submit feedback through the app, only the information you voluntarily provide will be shared. The feedback form clearly states what data is being submitted before sending.

Third-Party Services

Our application integrates third-party services to enhance functionality and provide a better user experience. These services may process certain non-personal data as necessary to operate their features. This includes, but is not limited to, services for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and other essential functionalities.

We do not collect or store sensitive payment details; all transactions are securely processed by the relevant platform provider.

How Is Your Data Stored?

  • All data is stored locally on your device and remains under your control.
  • Backups are only stored on your Google account if Google Play Store’s backup feature is enabled. We do not have access to this backup.
  • No personal or financial information is shared with us unless you explicitly choose to do so.

How Do We Ensure Your Data Security?

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data, ensuring that all financial records remain stored securely on your device. However, since data is not stored on our servers, we cannot restore lost data if you lose access to your device. We recommend keeping independent backups for additional security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. Any significant updates will be posted within the app.
